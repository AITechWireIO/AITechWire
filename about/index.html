How we work

Every article starts from sources fetched that day. We write the story from those sources, then check it against them claim by claim before it is published: every number, date and quote in a piece traces back to a named source. Our editorial tools are AI-assisted; the standard for what gets published is not. A thesis, the developments that support or complicate it, and a resolution, in plain English, with no hype.

New articles publish daily at 7 AM US Central, with a short version on X and LinkedIn.
