Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. The short version: we collect almost nothing, we sell nothing, and you can ask us to delete what little we hold.

Who we are

aitechwire.io is operated by Lexonia Group LLC, Minnesota, USA. Questions about this policy: info@aitechwire.io.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you subscribe to the daily brief. We use it to send you that email and nothing else. Unsubscribe any time by emailing info@aitechwire.io or using the link in any email we send.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Netlify) records standard technical data when a page is served, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and performance. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Email you send us. If you write to us, we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to answer and follow up.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set tracking cookies and we run no advertising trackers. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record the request under Google's privacy policy. Links to X, LinkedIn and other sites are governed by those sites' policies once you leave ours. If we add analytics in future, we will update this page and say so here.

How we share data

We never sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the service providers that run the site and send our email (such as our hosting and email providers), and only for that purpose, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Subscriber emails until you unsubscribe. Correspondence for as long as the matter is open. Server logs according to our hosting provider's retention schedule.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@aitechwire.io and we will respond within 30 days. Residents of the EU/EEA, the UK and California have additional rights under their local laws, which we honor.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
